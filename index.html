Measure your window with your phone camera

Point at the corners of your window or balcony and we'll work out how many meters of curtain fabric you need.

  1. Tap the top-left corner of the window frame
  2. Tap the top-right corner to get the width
  3. Tap the bottom-right corner to get the height
  4. Optional: tap the floor under the window for floor-length curtains

Works on Android phones with Chrome. Aim at the frame or the wall, not the glass — the camera can't detect transparent glass.
